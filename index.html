Interior Design Task

Instructions

This task is centered around a conversational assistant designed to help you find your most preferred interior design for a room.

To begin the interaction, please provide a short description of a design to the assistant.

Based on this description, the assistant will initialize a set of options. These will be presented to you as a query. You may proceed to answer these queries as you see fit.

After three turns have elapsed, an option to finalize your most recent choice as the design will be available. The number of turns is inconsequential to the task, and we encourage users to be idealistic in the manner they decide to finalize a design. Please choose as you would in real life!
